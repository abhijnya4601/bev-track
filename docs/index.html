    What this shows

      Data: nuScenes (Motional), CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Only box coordinates derived from it are shown here. Models: BEVFormer-tiny (camera) and CenterPoint (LiDAR), public checkpoints, both trained on nuScenes' official train split. Evaluation therefore uses only the mini scenes from the official val split (0103, 0553, 0796, 0916). All four are daytime and dry, so lighting and weather could not be evaluated without leakage. "Fusion" here is late fusion of the two models' finished boxes, not a jointly trained fusion network.
